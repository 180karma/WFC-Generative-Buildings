Wave function collapse lays modular rooms on a plot of up to 5×5 squares and four storeys, dresses them in one of twenty-eight building traditions from the Middle East, Asia, Europe, Africa, the Americas and the Pacific, or lays them out as one of ten ancient ruins, and lets you walk through the result.

Stairs

Each square is a module column, on a plot of up to 5 × 5. 0 leaves it open ground; 1–4 sets the most storeys the solver may stack there. Shift-click lowers. Inside builds a stair core against the wall opposite its entrance; Outside runs masonry flights up the outside walls to roof terraces and upper doors; Both keeps the core and adds outside flights where a wall allows. Module sets the width and length of every square: larger modules give roomier halls, wider courts and more windows per wall. In walk mode you can step out of upper doors onto sloped roofs and cross from roof to roof.
